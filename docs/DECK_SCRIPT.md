## Q&A one-liners

- *Trained on synthetic data?* "Yes, declared; the synthetic AUC proves the pipeline. The Berka result shows the method carries signal on real data; the pilot retrains inside the perimeter."
- *Why not the credit bureau?* "Thin-file borrowers have no bureau record; where one exists it is an input — see the obligations table."
- *Why not a tax certificate?* "Self-employed income is untaxed and unfiled; the regulation itself names account inflows as the evidence."
- *Family-programme loans are ПДН-exempt.* "Correct — cl.11, and the engine says so on screen. Repayment capacity still has to be assessed, and Ommabop 2, Express and Business Online 2 are in scope."
- *What if the statement is forged?* "Balance-chain, duplicates, round-sum spikes, circular transfers; and in production MKBANK's own account data comes from the core, not from an upload."
- *Is it just an LLM reading PDFs?* "There is no LLM in this product. Seven components; the only generative step is a template with a verifier."
- *Uzbek language?* "OCR in Uzbek Latin, Cyrillic and Russian, self-hosted; categoriser keywords in all three; memo in English for this jury, templates are trivially bilingual."
- *Personal data?* "Nothing leaves the device in the prototype; nothing leaves the bank in production; consent recorded in the case; retention is the bank's policy."
- *Cost per request?* "Zero marginal. Static hosting. The optional narrative LLM would add well under one cent on an on-prem GPU."
