## Q&A one-liners

- *Trained on synthetic data?* "Yes, declared; the synthetic AUC proves the pipeline. The Berka result shows the method carries signal on real data; the pilot retrains inside the perimeter."
- *Berka is only 443 loans.* "44 bad loans, 5-fold out-of-fold; the 95 % interval on the 0.90 AUC is about 0.86–0.94. The claim is 'cash-flow features carry signal on real statements', not a production number."
- *Why XGBoost if logistic regression is almost as good?* "On these features they are close, and we say so. We ship the trees for two properties the bank needs: monotone constraints the risk committee can sign off, and threshold effects — a cash buffer matters a lot below one month of income and little above. If the pilot shows no gain, logistic regression is one line to switch."
- *Anyone can recompute your hash chain in the browser.* "Yes — in the prototype the chain proves integrity, not provenance. In production the latest hash is written to the bank's database and signed by the SSO identity, so a later edit of the browser copy no longer matches the anchored one."
- *What about KATM, the credit bureau?* "KATM tells the bank what the borrower owes and how they repaid; Daromad proves what they earn. In production the obligations table is a KATM pull, and we cross-check it against repayments visible in the statement."
- *The 15 % allowance in the 2024 amendment?* "That is the only relief a bank gets — a quota of up to 15 % of the number of its loans to individuals, tracked at portfolio level by the risk function. The 50- and 75-BRV exclusions in the same clause are written for microfinance organisations and pawnshops. Daromad has a lender-type setting, defaults to bank, computes the ratio for every application and shows the quota as a note."
- *Guarantors — the press summary says they count as borrowers.* "The regulation text says co-borrowers always count, guarantors only when the guaranteed loan is more than 30 days overdue (cl.4); quotes are in docs/REGULATION_QUOTES.md."
- *Why not the credit bureau?* "Thin-file borrowers have no bureau record; where one exists it is an input — see the obligations table."
- *Two date columns — operation and posting date?* "The first date column is the operation date and that is the one we keep; the posting date is never read as a number. The fixture in the repo is a 25-page PDF with both columns, document numbers and 20-digit accounts: rows, totals and months are exact."
- *Why not a tax certificate?* "Self-employed income is untaxed and unfiled; the regulation itself names account inflows as the evidence."
- *Family-programme loans are ПДН-exempt.* "Correct — cl.11, and the engine says so on screen. Repayment capacity still has to be assessed, and Ommabop 2, Express and Business Online 2 are in scope."
- *What if the statement is forged?* "Balance-chain, duplicates, round-sum spikes, circular transfers; and in production MKBANK's own account data comes from the core, not from an upload."
- *Is it just an LLM reading PDFs?* "There is no LLM in this product. Seven components; the only generative step is a template with a verifier."
- *Uzbek language?* "OCR in Uzbek Latin, Cyrillic and Russian, self-hosted; categoriser keywords in all three; memo in English for this jury, templates are trivially bilingual."
- *Personal data?* "Nothing leaves the device in the prototype; nothing leaves the bank in production; consent recorded in the case; retention is the bank's policy."
- *Cost per request?* "Zero marginal. Static hosting. The optional narrative LLM would add well under one cent on an on-prem GPU."
